# Figures of cerda2003

The paper has no plots: every figure is a photograph or micrograph without axes, tick marks or scale
bars, so `figures.yaml` is empty. A second figure pass (2026-11-13) re-rendered each figure at 200 dpi
to look for axes, scale bars or plotted points and found none. No datum in `data.yaml` is a value read
by eye off a figure (every "~" or "≈" value is quoted from the text or a caption), so there is nothing
to replace with a calibrated reading. No measurement is plotted, so no figure can settle one of the
open questions.

- **Fig. 1 (p. 1), wrinkles in a stretched polyethylene sheet**: left out (photograph; the x and y
  arrows show directions only and have no ticks or scale). It is the natural check of
  `run_stretched_fig1`: the number of wrinkles across W ≈ 10 cm would give a wavelength to compare
  with the stretched-sheet form. Counting crests in a photograph scaled only by an approximate caption
  width ("W ≈ 10 cm", with perspective and clamped-edge curvature) is not a calibrated reading, so it is
  not entered; the paper itself defers the quantitative check to ref. 14.
- **Fig. 2(a) (p. 3), shriveled apple**: left out (photograph, no scale beyond "≈ 5 cm" in the caption).
- **Fig. 2(b) (p. 3), wrinkles on the back of a hand**: left out (photograph, no scale).
- **Fig. 3(a) (p. 4), wrinkles under a crawling cell**: left out (micrograph, no scale bar; the caption
  gives only "typical wavelengths in the range of um").
- **Fig. 3(b1), (b2) (p. 4), wrinkles on a sheared vesicle**: left out (micrographs, no scale bar; the
  arrows in (b1) show the shear direction only; the caption gives the vesicle size ≈ 10 um only).
