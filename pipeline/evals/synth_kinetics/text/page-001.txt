                 Rates of a Unimolecular Reaction and its Diffusion Limit
                                 A. N. Example
                 Journal of Synthetic Results 1, 1-2 (2026)

Abstract. We estimate the rate constant of a unimolecular reaction from the
Arrhenius law, its half-life, and the diffusion-limited rate of the reverse
association.

1. Rate constant. The rate constant follows the Arrhenius law

        k = A exp(-Ea / (R T))                                        (1)

where R = 8.314 J mol-1 K-1 is the gas constant. For the reaction studied
here the pre-exponential factor is A = 1.0 x 10^13 s^-1 and the activation
energy is Ea = 80 kJ/mol. At room temperature, T = 298 K, Eq. 1 gives

        k = 9.6 x 10^-1 s^-1.

2. Half-life. A first-order reaction has the half-life

        t1/2 = ln 2 / k                                               (2)

so that with the rate constant above the half-life is t1/2 = 7.3 s.
