3. Diffusion limit. The reverse association cannot be faster than the
Smoluchowski diffusion limit

        kD = 4 pi D R* NA                                             (3)

where D is the relative diffusion constant, R* the encounter radius and
NA = 6.022 x 10^23 mol^-1 is Avogadro's number. For two small molecules in
water, D = 1.0 x 10^-9 m^2/s, and taking a typical encounter radius we find

        kD = 7.6 x 10^9 M^-1 s^-1.

4. Conclusion. The reaction is slow compared with the diffusion limit.
