BoxGraph

Executable literature reviews

All models are wrong, but some are useful (George E. P. Box). In these reviews each paper's models are taken as stated and put to work, to see where each one holds.

Every value is traced to the page it was printed on. Every model is rebuilt, run on the paper's own inputs and compared with what the paper reports, at the precision it was printed with. Relations between the models of different papers are tested rather than assumed, and released code and data are checked against the paper they belong to. Nothing is corrected or overwritten: a disagreement is kept as a result, with the open question it raises.
